# Onboarding guide (for outside testers)

Five steps on X Layer mainnet, about 10 minutes. You keep every key. Page: [getfengce.vercel.app/onboard](https://getfengce.vercel.app/onboard/).

1. **Wallet.** OKX Wallet or MetaMask in your browser, a little OKB on X Layer for gas, and 1 to 2 USDT0.
2. **Safe.** Open the page, press **Connect**, pick your wallet, then **Create a new Safe** (one transaction).
3. **Limits.** On **Limits**, set max per buy and trades per hour, press **Test before you sign**, then **Install with one signature**.
4. **Try.** On **Try**, swap under your cap (allowed), then over it (refused, with the reason). **My wallet** shows both.
5. **Send it.** On the onboarding page press **Send my Safe address** (a prefilled GitHub issue), or send the address to
   whoever invited you.

Installs appear on [/docs, For judges](https://getfengce.vercel.app/docs/?tab=judges), read from the chain.
